Foundation of Faith

What We Believe

Our doctrines are centered on the timeless truths of Holy Scripture and the historic Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Scripture

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, serving as the supreme rule for faith and Christian living.

Grace

We believe salvation is God's free gift of grace, received through faith alone in Jesus Christ, and not by human works.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, who lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, and rose bodily from the dead.

Faith & Justification

Believers are justified by faith in Christ and called to live transformed, holy lives by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit indwells, guides, comforts, and equips every believer with spiritual gifts for Christian service.

Church Community

The church is the body of Christ called to worship God, nurture disciples, care for the needy, and spread the Gospel locally and globally.
