Donation & Tax Policy

BATU YEHIYWOT BERHAN CHURCH is a registered non-profit religious organization in Ethiopia. Contributions to the Building Fund are used strictly for land acquisition, architectural design, construction, and equipment for our new church facility.

Tax Deductibility

Donations may be tax-deductible according to regional laws and donor jurisdiction. Official receipts are issued via email for all completed gifts.

Refund Policy

Because charitable donations are immediately committed to building projects, gifts are non-refundable unless made in error. Please contact finance@hiwotbirhanchurch.org for assistance.
