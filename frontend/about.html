Sacred Milestones

From Chapa’s Night Sanctuary to Nationwide Awakening

Witness how God guided the Hiwot Birhan movement through every chapter of trial and triumph.

1960
Swedish Pioneers

Swedish Pentecostal Missionaries Arrive

Swedish Pentecostal missionaries arrived in Ethiopia with a deep passion for the Gospel, dedicating themselves to sowing the seeds of faith and serving local communities.

1962
First Chapel

Chapa: Built by Night Under Heavy Opposition

Missionary Ababa Dukemo established the first Hiwot Birhan church at Chapa. The first church building was constructed in a traditional style using wood and grass. Because local authorities strongly opposed the work, believers built it secretly under cover of night.

1964
Compassion & Healing

Wondo Genet: Brite Bergfelt Opens Clinic

Missionary and nurse Brite Bergfelt opened a medical clinic in Wondo Genet, caring for the sick while faithfully preaching the Gospel. A dedicated fellowship of new converts gathered around her selfless medical work.

1974–
1991
Underground Church

The Derg Regime: 17 Years Underground

The Marxist Derg regime came to power and launched brutal persecution against churches. Missionaries were expelled, church buildings closed, Bibles burned, and pastors and evangelists were imprisoned, tortured, and killed. The church was forced underground, meeting secretly for 17 years in homes while faith deepened and prayer multiplied.

1975
Denominational Unity

Awasa Mission Conference: Unified as One

The church was officially formed in 1975 at an annual mission conference in Awasa, where representatives agreed to establish the Hiwot Berhan Church as a unified body across Ethiopia.

1991
National Revival

Fall of the Derg: Emerging into the Light

After the Derg regime fell in 1991, the church emerged triumphantly from hiding and began to grow publicly again. Decades of covert prayer ignited nationwide revival, expanding across cities and towns.

1M+
Today
1 Million Believers

One of Ethiopia's Largest Pentecostal Denominations

Today, the Hiwot Berhan Church is one of the largest Pentecostal denominations in Ethiopia, with over one million members. Batu Hiwot Birhan Church in Batu Zeway carries this sacred legacy into the future!

Ziway / Batu, Ethiopia

Batu Hiwot Birhan: Carrying the Sacred Torch

Batu Hiwot Birhan Church in Batu Zeway is part of this movement. It carries the same legacy of faith, perseverance, and hope that started with those early missionaries and survived through years of persecution.

Our congregation reflects the rich diversity of our region, praising God together in Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and English. We are dedicated to preaching the uncompromised Gospel, discipling believers of all ages, and reaching our city with God's transforming love.

Theological Pillars

Full Statement of Faith

1. The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible, consisting of 66 books of the Old and New Testaments, is the fully inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God.

2. The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three equal Persons: God the Father, God the Son (Jesus Christ), and God the Holy Spirit.

3. Salvation by Grace Through Faith

We believe that all humans need salvation, which is received solely by God's grace through faith in the crucified and risen Lord Jesus Christ alone.

4. Living Hope & Eternity

We look forward with joy to Christ's return, the resurrection of believers, and eternal fellowship with our Creator in glory.
